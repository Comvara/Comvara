## What we do

- **Custom web systems**: CRMs, portals, dashboards, admin panels and internal tools, with no templates and no page-builder limits.
- **E-commerce**: fully custom stores with Paystack checkout, delivery-zone pricing, order tracking and admin dashboards.
- **AI-powered solutions**: AI chat assistants, AI-assisted ticketing and email composers built into real products.

## Selected work

### BizTrack: CRM and invoicing SaaS for small businesses
Our own product, live at [app.comvara.com.ng](https://app.comvara.com.ng). Multi-tenant, with client and pipeline management, follow-up tracking, invoicing with branded PDF receipts, content planning, an AI email composer, WhatsApp integration and Paystack subscriptions.
`React` `Vite` `PHP` `MySQL` `Paystack` `PWA`

### Aria: white-label AI customer assistant
Our AI product for Nigerian SMEs, live at [aria.comvara.com.ng](https://aria.comvara.com.ng). A business drops one embed script onto its website and gets an AI assistant that answers customer questions around the clock, styled to match its brand. It comes with a client dashboard, conversation history, per-client branding, Paystack subscription billing and an admin panel for signups, plans and revenue. It is already running on live client websites.
`Node.js` `Express` `Paystack` `AI`

### OFZcouture: custom e-commerce store
A womenswear brand's online store, built with no CMS and no templates. It has a 3-step Paystack checkout, a delivery-zone calculator covering all 36 states, live search, wishlist, order tracking, promo codes, multi-photo and video products, and a 6-tab admin dashboard. It also includes SEO, Open Graph and Schema.org markup, and WebP image compression.
`PHP` `MySQL` `JavaScript` `Paystack` `Meta Pixel`

### Pulse Logistics: logistics platform
A live platform for a Nigerian logistics company with hubs in Lagos and Port Harcourt. It includes a pricing engine (volumetric weight, weight brackets, handling fees, urgency and insurance), shipment tracking, rider assignment, SMS and WhatsApp notifications, an AI chat assistant, and a hardened API with rate limiting and JWT auth. Automated nightly backups and auto-restart are in place.
`React` `Node.js` `Express` `MySQL` `Termii` `AI`

### Elan Management Services: corporate website and HRM platform
A digital transformation build for a Nigerian manpower and outsourcing company. It includes a corporate website and a custom HR management platform with employee records, departments and pay grades, leave management, recruitment, client contracts, a self-service staff portal and role-based permissions. Branded documents export as Word and PDF. We also set up the company's business email with custom DNS and added our Aria AI assistant to the site.
`Node.js` `Express` `MySQL` `JWT` `HTML` `CSS` `JavaScript`

### AI-powered complaints management system
A helpdesk-style platform modelled on tools like Zendesk and Freshdesk. It offers ticket tracking, role-based access, email notifications with SMTP fallback, analytics and AI-assisted responses.
`PHP` `MySQL` `PHPMailer` `AI`

### School result management portal
A results portal for a school in Enugu, with student login, an admin panel, bulk PDF result uploads and bulk student CSV import. We also built the school's main website, a 9-page mobile-first site.
`PHP` `MySQL` `HTML` `CSS` `JavaScript`

### Mandate247: WordPress site and marketing strategy
An end-to-end build for a civic advocacy organisation, including a supporter portal, a candidate directory, an aspirant assessment form and a WooCommerce store with Paystack. The work also covered the platform-by-platform digital marketing strategy.
`WordPress` `WooCommerce` `Paystack` `Kadence`
